Fantasy Privacy Policy

Fantasy is a fantasy football squad-builder app. This policy explains how the
app handles information.

What Fantasy collects

Fantasy does not require an account and does not collect personal information
for a remote service. It has no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no
third-party SDKs that collect or transmit data. Fantasy does not send squad
data, settings, or usage data to a server.

Where your data is stored

Your squad, gameweek progress, settings, and onboarding preference are stored
in Fantasy's private app storage on your device. This information stays in the
app's private storage and is not shared with us or other companies. Fantasy
uses open-source Flutter packages for its interface and device-local storage;
these packages are not used to provide advertising, analytics, accounts, or
cloud storage.

Import and export

Fantasy includes an optional squad export/import action. If you choose to
export, the app places the squad data in your device clipboard so you can save
or move it yourself. Clipboard contents are controlled by your device and are
not sent to Fantasy. Only use export with a destination you trust.

Deletion

Uninstalling Fantasy deletes its locally stored app data from the device. You
can also reset progress from the app's settings. Any copy you deliberately
exported outside Fantasy, such as a clipboard or notes copy, must be deleted
separately by you.

Children

Fantasy does not knowingly collect information from children or from any
other user. It has no account or communication feature.
